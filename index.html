Your Climate, Your Hometown in 2080

i Project Write-Up

So far we've implemented and stylized a GitHub site with 3 different sections. The first asks the user some questions that gauge their opinions on climate change. Next they can select a city and it shows the increase in warm days in 2080 based on the climate scenario pertaining to their answer choices. There are visuals showing the world increase spread, the warm days across the year in your scenario, and a graph showing the predicted increase in the hot season for 3 different climate scenarios. At the bottom there's a slider that can let you change between the 3 different scenarios and this alters the data shown above.

The most challenging aspect of our projects design will be implementing our vision for the final UI, layout, and animations. We want to be able to have the user explore a globe, similar to google earth, where you can pan and zoom to see the different changes across earths warm days. We are expecting this to be challenging to animate smoothly, there is a fear of optimization/frame rate issues. Also we have to figure out how to incorporate our storytelling idea where we ask questions before showing, and let it mix well with a giant globe that will be on the site.
